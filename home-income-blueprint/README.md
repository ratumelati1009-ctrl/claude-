## About this book

A complete modern reimagining of dated "work from home" material into a warm, practical **workbook for anyone starting a side hustle or home business today** — freelancing, digital products, online tutoring, local services, or a small shop. It follows the launch journey from first idea to sustainable income.

**Every chapter includes:**
- A short true-to-life **story**
- A plain-language **explanation**
- A hands-on **exercise**
- A tick-off **checklist**
- A usable **worksheet** (planner, scorecard, tracker)
- A **"Reality Check"** box with honest expectations
