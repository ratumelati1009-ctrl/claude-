## About this book

A complete reimagining of generic organic-gardening material into a warm, practical **workbook for small-space growers** — renters, apartment dwellers, and anyone with a sunny balcony, patio, or windowsill. It follows the growing journey from empty pot to full plate.

**Every chapter includes:**
- A short true-to-life **story**
- A plain-language **explanation**
- A gentle **exercise**
- A tick-off **checklist**
- A usable **worksheet** (planner, care log, harvest record)
- A **"Grow With Kids"** family box
