# The Busy Parent's Natural Reset

**Lose Fat for Good Without Gyms, Pills, or Crash Diets — A 30-Minute-a-Day Plan Built Around Real Family Life**

A practical natural-weight-loss workbook for busy parents, with explanations, real-life examples, exercises, worksheets, and checklists.

## What's inside

- **Part 1 — Understand:** why weight sneaks up, fat vs. weight, the one simple law of fat loss, finding your starting numbers
- **Part 2 — Move:** movement that fits a chaotic day, a no-gym activity menu
- **Part 3 — Eat & Drink:** water habits, building better plates, natural helpers
- **Part 4 — Sustain:** habits that survive busy weeks, a 4-week reset plan
- **Appendices:** superfoods shopping list, negative-calorie foods, printable worksheets & trackers
