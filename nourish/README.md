## About this book

A complete reimagining of generic pregnancy-nutrition material into a warm, practical, **workbook-style companion** for first-time expectant mothers *and their partners*. It follows the pregnancy journey trimester by trimester.

**Every chapter includes:**
- A short true-to-life **story**
- A plain-language **explanation**
- A gentle **exercise / reflection**
- A tick-off **checklist**
- A usable **worksheet** (meal planner, symptom tracker, etc.)
- A **"For the Partner"** action box

## Contents

- **Part 1 — Foundations:** The Only Five Rules You Really Need · Eating for Two (What That Actually Means)

- **Part 3 — Second Trimester:** Protein and Your Growing Baby · Cravings, Weight, and Balance
